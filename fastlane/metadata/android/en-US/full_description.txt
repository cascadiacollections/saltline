Salt Line is a single-player artillery roguelike. Aim over procedural ridges and chasms, reshape the terrain with every impact, and see how far your shell supply can carry you.

Each screen is generated from a shareable seed. Plan your angle, power, and shot type; clear targets efficiently to conserve shells. Every four screens, choose a modifier that changes how your run plays. The horizon keeps moving until your ammunition runs out.

Features:
• Deterministic procedural terrain and shareable seeds
• Destructible ground that remembers every crater during a run
• A growing set of artillery rounds and run modifiers
• Touch controls, including buttons and optional drag aiming
• Controller support, plus remappable keyboard controls
• Local plain-text saves and replayable runs
• No ads, in-app purchases, network connection, or telemetry

Salt Line is free and open-source software, released under the MIT License. It is an early development preview; gameplay and balance may change.
